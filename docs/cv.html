Profile

Biostatistics leader pairing hands-on industry development and FDA regulatory experience across the drug-development lifecycle with a strong academic and methodological foundation. Currently Senior Manager, Biostatistics at Pfizer (Associate Director–equivalent) and a member of the Data Science & Analytics India leadership team, leading a team of statisticians across global Oncology and Inflammation & Immunology programs. Former FDA Oncology Center of Excellence primary reviewer with hands-on NDA/BLA experience, product-labelling negotiation, and pioneering use of real-world evidence and external-control cohorts.

Research focus

Much of my recent work sits in two places: using target trial emulation to bring observational data to bear on questions trials answer slowly or not at all, and endpoint and estimand strategy — deciding what a trial should measure before arguing about how to analyse it.
